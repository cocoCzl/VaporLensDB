# Code of Conduct

VaporLensDB contributors must help keep the project welcoming, professional,
and safe. Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, focus disagreement on
ideas and code, and do not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats, or
the publication of another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior violates these expectations. Report conduct issues privately through
GitHub's repository security advisory form. Reports will be reviewed promptly
and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly
representing VaporLensDB. It is based on the Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
